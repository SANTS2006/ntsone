# NTSOne video series: storyboard

Style: premium SaaS demo. Brand blue-cyan gradient, white cards, Poppins-style type, light theme for the app screens,
dark gradient title cards. Real app screen recordings for every demonstration; motion graphics only for titles,
transitions, callouts and the opening and closing. No AI-generated interfaces.

Audio: friendly, confident English voice-over, subtle instrumental bed 18 dB under the voice, burned-in and `.srt` subtitles.
Formats: 1920x1080 (16:9) masters, 1080x1920 (9:16) social cuts, 30-60 second promos.

## Video 2: Getting started (5:00)
1. Open the site, tour the home page. 2. Sign up: organization name, email code, terms. 3. Sign in. 4. Dashboard tour. 5. Sidebar sections.
6. Settings: profile photo, organization name. 7. Light/dark mode, installing the app on a phone. 8. Where to get help (contact, report).

## Video 3: Registrations, ID cards and tickets (12:00)
Create program, cover image and status (1) / form builder: add questions, sections, rules, layout, consent, preview (3) /
publish and share link and QR (1) / fill the public form: inline validation, review, submit (2) / registrations table:
search, filters, export (1) / detail: answers, status change, history (1) / import from Excel (1) / ID card and ticket
designs, check-in, Verifications log (2).

## Video 4: Voting polls (8:00)
Create poll and the "voters need an account?" choice / ballot builder / settings (one vote per email, domain limit, verified
voters) / open the poll / voter journey: sign up, email code, vote / guest voting without accounts / live results, voters list,
exports / paid voting: price, exact amounts, fee agreement, voter payment, choose candidate.

## Video 5: Businesses (10:00)
Create business / order form items with pictures / live order page: browse, cart, checkout, review, confirm / order list,
statuses, customer emails / invoice from scratch, layouts, PDF, email / receipt / quotation / business card / analytics.

## Video 6: Payments (8:00)
Turn payments on and agree to the fee (checkbox behaviour) / payer experience up to Monime / payments tab, refund, waive,
paid in person / payment fund: balances, hold, all-time cards / add withdrawal account / withdraw / automatic withdrawal /
ledger and withdrawals tables / cancel flow and order status.

## Video 7: Bookkeeping, contributions, wish lists (8:00)
Cash book big buttons, credit, loans, reports, voice note / contribution list: monthly records and locking, balance /
wish list: add items, mark gotten, summary, share link and QR.

## Video 8: Team, settings, security (5:00)
Invite a team member, give access per item, roles, suspend / settings: change email, change password / leave an
organization / delete account (explained, not performed) / sign-in protections, password rules.

## Video 9: Platform team (7:00)
Organizations and detail, per-organization payment terms, platform payments summary, review queue, platform fund,
inbox, audit log, maintenance mode, backups.

## Closing (all videos)
Recap of benefits, "this video is also your user guide", logo, website, contact.

## Recording rules
- Demo organization and fictitious people only; never real customer data, secrets or the production database.
- Never perform irreversible or real-money actions; payment pages are shown up to the redirect or in a sandbox.
- Blur or crop any email address, token or password that appears.
