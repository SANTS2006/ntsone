# NTSOne user guide

This guide follows the training videos. It describes only what the system does today.

## 1. Getting started
1. Open the NTSOne website and choose **Sign up**. Enter your organization's name, your name, email and a strong password, agree to the terms, and enter the 6-character code emailed to you.
2. Sign in at `/login`. Forgotten password: **Forgot password** emails a code.
3. The **Dashboard** summarizes your programs, polls, businesses, payments and books. The sidebar opens each module.
4. **Settings** holds your profile photo, organization name, email, password, other organizations you belong to (with a Leave button) and **Delete account**.

## 2. Registrations (Programs)
1. **Programs, Create program**: name, description, cover image. The program starts as a draft.
2. **Form**: add questions (23 types), group them into sections, set rules (show, require, limit, pre-fill), choose step-by-step or single page, and preview on desktop, tablet and mobile. **Publish** when ready.
3. Share the public link or QR code. Registrants fill the form, see mistakes under each question (red, then green when right), review their answers and submit.
4. **Registrations**: search, filter by status, payment and date, export to Excel or CSV, open a registration to read answers and files, change its status (the registrant can be emailed) and see its history.
5. **Import** from Excel, CSV or a Word table to add or update many registrations.
6. **ID cards and tickets**: pick a design, colours and logo; registrants download theirs. A QR code can be scanned at the door; every scan appears in **Verifications**.

## 3. Voting polls
1. **Polls, Create poll.** Decide whether voters need an account. With accounts, voters sign up and confirm their email; you may limit email domains or use a verified list. Without accounts, anyone with the link can vote and each browser counts as one voter.
2. **Ballot**: add positions and candidates with photos. Open the poll when ready.
3. Voters choose a candidate per position. The ballot is secret: you see who voted, never for whom.
4. **Results** update live; export results and the voters list.
5. **Paid voting**: set a price per vote, choose a fixed or any amount (amounts must be exact multiples of the price), tick the fee agreement and save.

## 4. Businesses
1. **Businesses, Create business**: logo, brand colour, contact details.
2. **Order form**: add items with pictures and prices. Share the order page; customers browse, add to their cart, check out, review and confirm.
3. **Orders**: track each order with your own statuses; customers can be emailed at each step.
4. **Invoices, receipts, quotations**: choose a layout, add items, tax and discounts, download a PDF or email it. **Business cards**: two-sided designs with a QR code.
5. **Analytics** shows orders, money invoiced, received and outstanding.

## 5. Payments
1. Under a program, order form or poll, open **Payments**, switch payments on, set prices and tick **I agree** to the platform fee (organization admins only). The box stays ticked after saving and must be ticked again after any change.
2. Payers pay with Orange Money, Africell Money or QMoney on Monime's page. A payment counts only after Monime confirms the full amount. If someone cancels, the order is marked cancelled. "We received your order" emails are sent only after payment.
3. **Payment fund** (admins): balances, total received all time (before the fee, after the fee, after refunds), withdrawal accounts, **Withdraw** and **Automatic withdrawal**. Everything appears in **Withdrawals** and **Money in and out**.
4. **Refunds** are available a few minutes after a payment; large ones wait for platform approval.

## 6. Bookkeeping, contributions and wish lists
- **Bookkeeping** (inside a business): big buttons for sales, spending, stock, money in and out, lending and borrowing; credit and loans; profit and loss, cash flow, who owes what; voice notes. Mistakes are voided with a reason, never deleted.
- **Contributions**: a list of contributors with monthly payments (a recorded month locks once it has passed), amounts taken out and the balance held.
- **Wish lists**: add items, mark them gotten with the price paid, see summaries, and share a private link or QR with view or edit access.

## 7. Team
Admins invite people, give each access to specific programs, polls and businesses (admin or viewer), suspend or remove them. Program admins manage only what they were given; viewers only see it.

## 8. For the platform team (super admin)
Organizations and their users, per-organization payment terms (fee, hold time, withdrawal start), all-organization payment summary, approvals of large withdrawals and refunds, the platform fund, the inbox, the audit log, maintenance mode and backups.

## 9. Tips
- Preview your form on mobile before publishing.
- Test the whole journey with a small payment before announcing a paid event.
- Keep your withdrawal account details right: new accounts wait a few minutes before first use.
- Give people the lowest role that lets them do their job.
